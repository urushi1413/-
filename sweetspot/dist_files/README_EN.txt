SWEET SPOT — real tennis where contact point and spin matter

STARTING THE GAME
  Double-click SweetSpot.exe. Keep the "sounds" and "fonts" folders next to the exe.
  Settings are saved to sweetspot_settings.txt in the same folder.

CONTROLS (IN A MATCH)
  WASD ............ move
  Mouse ........... aim (direction and depth)
  Left click ...... topspin (hold longer for more depth, release to hit)
  Right click ..... slice
  Space + click ... flat
  Shift + click ... lob
  Ctrl + click .... drop shot
  Q ............... crouch (for low balls)
  X ............... smash your racket (between points). You get FIRED UP for a few points:
                    faster and harder, but more errors. Point penalty from the 3rd time
  E ............... pump up the crowd (they roar if you just won the point)
  Z ............... swat the ball into the crowd (code violation, counted like racket abuse)
  R ............... after a point: replay (C switches camera)
  Tab ............. stats
  F3 .............. photo mode (hides the HUD and changes the camera; press again for the next)
  F1 / F2 ......... screen info / guides
  M ............... mute
  Esc ............. menu

KEYBOARD ONLY (NO MOUSE)
  J / K / L ....... topspin / slice / flat (serve: kick / slice / flat). Hold Shift for a lob, Ctrl for a drop shot
  U / O / I / , ... aim left / right / deeper / shorter

CHANGING KEYS
  SETTINGS > Keys. Select an action, press Enter (or click), then press the new key.
  If another action used that key, the two are swapped. "Reset to defaults" puts everything back.

FIRST TIME?
  Choose HOW TO PLAY on the title screen: move, hit, aim and serve in 4 short steps.

SERVING
  Left click kick / Right click slice / Space + click flat
  Press to toss, release to hit. Release around the top of the toss for a good serve.
  Keep holding to let a bad toss drop and toss again.

MODES
  Match / Rally practice / Return practice / Serve practice
  Grand Slam: a 16-player knockout, or a season of 4 slams in a row.
  Choose the AI level (Easy / Normal / Pro) and style (Casual / Real).
  New players: try Casual with an Easy AI.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit), graphics with OpenGL 3.3, a mouse
