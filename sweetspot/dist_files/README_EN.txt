SWEET SPOT — real tennis where contact point and spin matter

STARTING THE GAME
  Double-click SweetSpot.exe. Keep the "sounds" and "fonts" folders next to the exe.
  Settings are saved to sweetspot_settings.txt in the same folder.

CONTROLS (IN A MATCH)
  WASD ............ move
  Mouse ........... aim (direction and depth)
  Left click ...... topspin (hold longer for more depth, release to hit)
  Right click ..... slice
  Space + click ... flat
  Shift + click ... lob
  Ctrl + click .... drop shot
  Q ............... crouch (for low balls)
  X ............... smash your racket (between points; penalties from the 2nd time)
  R ............... after a point: replay (C switches camera)
  Tab ............. stats
  F1 / F2 ......... screen info / guides
  M ............... mute
  Esc ............. menu

SERVING
  Left click kick / Right click slice / Space + click flat
  Press to toss, release to hit. Release around the top of the toss for a good serve.
  Keep holding to let a bad toss drop and toss again.

MODES
  Match / Rally practice / Return practice / Serve practice
  Grand Slam: a 16-player knockout, or a season of 4 slams in a row.
  Choose the AI level (Easy / Normal / Pro) and style (Casual / Real).
  New players: try Casual with an Easy AI.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit), graphics with OpenGL 3.3, a mouse
